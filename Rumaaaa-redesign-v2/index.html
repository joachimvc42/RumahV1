Questions

The honest FAQ.

Anything we didn't cover? Drop us a message — we usually answer the same day.

Can a foreigner own land in Lombok?
Not directly under freehold (Hak Milik), but freehold-like exposure exists through long leaseholds (Hak Sewa / Hak Pakai) or via a properly structured PT PMA. We walk you through which structure fits your goal, time horizon and tax situation — without pretending one size fits all.
Do you take a commission from owners?
We work on a transparent, flat coordination fee paid by the client — not a percentage commission from the seller. That removes the incentive to push you toward the most expensive deal.
How long does a typical rental signature take?
Once we've shortlisted and visited together, most long-term leases are signed within 5 to 10 working days, including translation and notary.
Can you manage a villa for me after I buy it?
Yes — optional property management (maintenance, staff, cleaning, owner reporting, short-term rental coordination) is one of our core services and the reason many investors come back.
What if I'm not in Lombok yet?
Most of our clients start the process remotely. We do live video walkthroughs, share full document packs, and only fly you over once we have a real shortlist worth your trip.
